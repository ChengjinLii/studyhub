StudyHub Bot hat assets
=======================

The SVGs in this directory are simple local drawings created for StudyHub.
They were visually cross-checked against public-domain vector reference sites
such as Openclipart and PublicDomainVectors for common hat silhouettes, but are
not runtime dependencies and are not direct copies.

Reference search targets:
- https://openclipart.org/
- https://publicdomainvectors.org/
